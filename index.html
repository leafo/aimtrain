Gyro Aim Trainer

Aim with the mouse (or gyro-as-mouse), fire with your gamepad.

Hold your phone sideways like a gamepad. Aim by moving it, tap anywhere to fire.

pointer lock only

Gamepad fire clicks whatever is under the crosshair, so the whole menu works from the controller. Start = start / pause, X = reload, Back = menu, Esc = pause. “System” uses the real OS cursor (lowest latency, but stops at the screen edge). “Pointer lock” draws its own crosshair from raw relative motion so it never hits the edge, at the cost of a little display latency. If Steam Input maps your trigger to a mouse click, keep “Mouse click fires” on.

Tap anywhere to fire; hold your finger down in tracking and weapon modes. Tap the top bar to pause. Reloads are automatic. “Pointer” aims where the back of the phone points (turn it left/right, tilt it up/down); “Steering wheel” rolls it for left/right instead. If the crosshair moves the wrong way, use Invert.
